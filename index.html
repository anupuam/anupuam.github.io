Welcome

I am an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Mathematics & Computing at NIT Jalandhar. My research focuses on Optimization, Evolutionary Algorithms, Explainable AI (xAI), and Large Language Models. I completed my PhD from IIT Roorkee in 2013 under the supervision of Prof. Kusum Deep.
