Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes · A classifier can be a flowchart

Growing a decision tree in Python

The maths

At each step the tree tries every question and keeps the one that leaves the purest piles: the fewest mistakes.

Gini = 1 − (share ripe)² − (share not ripe)²

  1. “Soft?” Yes pile: 7 ripe, 1 not ripe
