Biography

Dr Anupam Yadav is an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Mathematics & Computing at Dr BR Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar. He received his PhD in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in 2013, specialising in Optimization, Swarm Intelligence, and Evolutionary Algorithms under the guidance of Prof. Kusum Deep. He has held visiting researcher positions at Korea University (Seoul), University of Jaen (Spain), Yonsei University (Seoul), and Kyungpook National University (Daegu, South Korea).

His current research interests span Optimization, Evolutionary Algorithms, Explainable AI (xAI), and Large Language Models. He is the Principal Investigator of a SERB-DST Core Research Grant (MATRICS) on projection algorithms for quantum computation (2022–2025) and serves as Managing Editor of the International Journal of Computational Intelligence Systems.
