Research Interests

Optimization Evolutionary Algorithms Swarm Intelligence Explainable AI (xAI) Large Language Models Metaheuristics Convex Feasibility Problems Quantum Computation

My primary research focuses on designing and analysing metaheuristic optimisation algorithms—particularly swarm-based and evolutionary approaches—and studying their theoretical properties. Recently I have extended this work to Explainable AI (xAI) for evolutionary algorithms and to studying the intersection of Large Language Models and evolutionary computation for automated algorithm design.
